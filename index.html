Dataset Scope & Explicit Limitations (Brief B Requirement)

✅ What This Dataset DOES Establish

  • Current Ambient Air Quality Index (AQI) based on US EPA & European standard calculation models.
  • Near-Real-Time Atmospheric Concentrations of PM2.5, PM10, $NO_2$, $O_3$, $SO_2$, and CO.
  • Immediate Health Risk Classification for general public and sensitive demographic groups.
  • Short-Term 24-Hour Trend Exposure Predictions for ambient urban outdoor areas.

❌ What This Dataset DOES NOT Establish

  • Long-Term Decadal Climate Trends (dataset provides short-term 7-day operational telemetry, not historical climate scale).
  • Indoor Air Quality or Ventilation Safety (measurements reflect outdoor ambient grid interpolation).
  • Block-Level Hyperlocal Micro-Variations (data grid resolution is interpolated at $11\text{km}$ cell spacing).
  • Medical Diagnostic or Epidemiological Causality (AQI scores indicate relative environmental risk, not direct medical outcomes).
